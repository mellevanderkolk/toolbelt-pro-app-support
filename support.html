Before requesting support

  • Confirm that all dimensions and weights are positive values.
  • Check that Metric or Imperial matches your source measurements.
  • Remember that the planner models identical items in an orthogonal grid; it is not free-form multi-SKU 3D packing.

Request support

Open a support request in the public issue tracker. Do not include passwords, access keys, personal documents, or other sensitive information.
